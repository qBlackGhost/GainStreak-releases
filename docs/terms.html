You can report or block any user from the Friends page and your inbox. Blocking takes effect straight away. Reports go to the developer and are reviewed, normally within 24 hours. The person you report isn't told who reported them. You can also write to gainstreak.app@gmail.com.

Content that breaks these rules can be removed, and the account behind it can be suspended or deleted, usually without warning when the problem is serious. A suspended account can't sign in and isn't visible to anyone. Workouts stay on your device. If you think a suspension is a mistake, write to the address above.
