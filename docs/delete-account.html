Delete your GainStreak account

Last updated 29 October 2026

In the app

  1. Open GainStreak and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Account → Delete account.
  3. Enter your password and tap Delete everything.

The account is deleted at once. It can't be undone.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app or can't sign in, email gainstreak.app@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my GainStreak account" and your username. The account is deleted within 30 days, and you'll get a reply when it's done. If you write from another address, you may be asked to confirm that the account is yours.

What's deleted

Everything the server holds about you:

  • Your account: username, email address and password hash
  • Your signed-in sessions on every device
  • Your friendships and friend requests
  • Your points, level and streak on the leaderboard
  • Your gym check-ins
  • Splits and workouts you've shared, or that are waiting for you
  • Feedback you've sent and the error reports attached to it
  • The people you've blocked, and reports you've sent or that were sent about you

Deleting in the app also removes your workouts, body data and everything else stored on that device. These were never on the server, so on other devices where you used GainStreak they stay until you uninstall the app there.

What's kept

  • Server logs, which include IP addresses, until Google Cloud removes them, at most 30 days later.
  • Feedback and report notifications already posted to the developer's private Discord channel. Ask in your email and they'll be removed too.

Nothing else is kept, and no backup is used to bring an account back.
